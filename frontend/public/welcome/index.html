From placement quiz to your next topic

Three steps, in this order. You can stop after any question and pick up where you left off.

01

Set your goals

Tell LearnPath what you are preparing for, how many hours a week you have, and how well you think you know DSA.

02

Take the placement quiz

32 questions, two per topic, one at a time. “I don't know this yet” counts as wrong, so a lucky guess never marks a topic as mastered.

03

Read your board

Your level, every topic's mastery, and which topics are open now, each with the reason in one sentence.

Every locked topic says what it waits for

The rule that locks a topic also writes its reason, so the board can never say one thing and do another. Harder questions count for more when mastery is scored.

Questions

What does it cost?
Nothing. LearnPath is a final-year B.Tech project at AKGEC Ghaziabad.
Which subjects does it cover?
Data Structures and Algorithms for now: 16 topics from Big-O to dynamic programming, with 48 questions.
How is my level worked out?
80% from your quiz score, where harder questions weigh more, and 20% from how you rate yourself. Under 40% is beginner, over 75% advanced.
Does it work on my phone?
Yes. The quiz and the board are built for phone screens as well as laptops.
Who built it?
Navya Mittal, Sakshi Shukla and Sarvesh Singh, B.Tech IT, guided by Ms. Ayushi.
